About ClearVoting

An independent record of how members of Congress vote, in plain English, with party labels hidden until you ask.

Why we built this

We built ClearVoting because we couldn't find a simple way to see how our representatives actually vote. The records are public, but they are buried in government websites and written in legal language.

Democracy works better when everyone can follow what Congress does. So ClearVoting takes the official voting record and shows it in plain language, without spin and without telling you what to think.

Who runs it

What we don't do

  • No scores, rankings or endorsements of anyone.
  • Party labels are hidden until you ask.
  • No opinions on bills or on the people who vote on them.
  • No ads, and we don't sell data.
  • We don't tell you how to vote — we show you how they voted.
