Methodology & Corrections

How ClearVoting gets its facts, what AI writes, the rules it follows, and what we have corrected. Every count on this page is taken from our data when the page is served.

Where the data comes from

Members, bills and House roll calls come from the Congress.gov API. Senate roll calls come from the Senate's XML records on Senate.gov. Campaign-finance totals come from the Federal Election Commission (FEC.gov). ZIP codes and Senate seats come from two public files, described under ZIP codes and districts and Seats on the ballot.

An automated sync collects the data once a week.

How fresh it is

Roll calls and bills are synced weekly; the edition line on every page shows the last sync and the newest roll call.

Every block written by AI says when the plain-English summaries last changed.
