At a glance

How Schumer has voted

Schumer votes most often on economics and public finance bills, where his record is split nearly evenly between votes in favor and against. On armed forces and national security bills, he votes in favor at a much higher rate than against. He also leans in favor on international affairs, transportation, and science and technology bills.

Read the rest

He voted in favor of bills to fund the government, cap insulin costs under Medicare, and set defense policies. He voted against bills that would have removed federal protections for certain wildlife species and canceled rules on gender identity in food programs.
