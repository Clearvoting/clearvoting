H.R. 6500Passed both chambersHouse Sept 1 · Senate Aug 8, 2026

Extend no-tax imports from 32 African countries through 2028

  • Removes import taxes on goods from 32 African countries through December 31, 2028
  • Extends customs fees and processing fees through December 31, 2031
  • Gives refunds for import taxes paid on African goods that entered the US after September 30, 2025
  • Lets African countries use fabric from China and other non-African countries and still avoid import taxes
