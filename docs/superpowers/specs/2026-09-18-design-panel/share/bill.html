What this bill does

We haven't written a plain-English summary for this bill yet. Summaries exist for bills acted on through June 30, 2026. Below is the official summary from the Congressional Research Service, unedited.

This bill prohibits institutions of higher education (IHEs) that participate in federal student aid programs from engaging in boycotts against Israel. Specifically, IHEs may not engage in a commercial action intended to limit commercial relations with Israel, or with an entity licensed by, regulated by, or organized under the laws of Israel…
