In summary

Written by AI from the record · Jun 13, 2026
Schumer votes most often on economics and public finance bills, where his record is split nearly evenly between votes in favor and against. On armed forces and national security bills, he votes in favor at a much higher rate than against… Read the summary

Schumer votes most often on economics and public finance bills, where his record is split nearly evenly between votes in favor and against. On armed forces and national security bills, he votes in favor at a much higher rate than against. He also leans in favor on international affairs, transportation, and science and technology bills. He voted in favor of bills to fund the government, cap insulin costs under Medicare, and set defense policies. He voted against bills that would have removed federal protections for certain wildlife species and canceled rules on gender identity in food programs.

Votes most often on: Economics and Public Finance · International Affairs · Armed Forces and National Security · Transportation and Public Works · Science, Technology, Communications
